The Last Blade has begun development

September 28, 2026

Phantom Pixel has officially started development of The Last Blade, a 2D pixel-art dark fantasy action platformer.

The game follows Elgart, a young knight who sets out to rescue Princess Eliana from the ancient dragon Vargras.

The first part will focus on platforming, sword combat, exploration, short cinematic scenes and a final battle against the dragon.
