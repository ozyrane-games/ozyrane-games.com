WHO WE ARE

A small studio.

We are currently at the beginning of our journey, learning, experimenting and building our first projects from the ground up.

Aethern I

The beginning of a new world.

Aethern I is an independent 2D game project currently being developed with Python and Pygame.

The project combines exploration, combat, characters and an original fantasy world. The goal is not simply to create a game, but to build a world that can grow with future projects.
