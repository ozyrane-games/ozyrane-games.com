ABOUT THE STUDIO

Small studio. Original ideas.

Ozyrane Games is a young independent game studio built around one simple idea: create our own games and worlds.

We are at the beginning of our journey, learning, experimenting and developing our first projects. Every game is an opportunity to learn something new about programming, gameplay and worldbuilding.

Our focus is on creating original experiences rather than following a fixed formula.

OZYRANE GAMES AETHERN I
PROJECT 01 2026 — PRESENT

Aethern I

The beginning of a new world.

Aethern I is an independent 2D fantasy game currently being developed with Python and Pygame.

The project combines exploration, combat, characters and an original fantasy setting. It is being developed step by step as both a game and the foundation of a larger world.
