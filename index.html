The Last Blade

The Last Blade is a short atmospheric 2D action-platformer set in a dark fantasy world.

The game follows a young knight who is sent on a dangerous journey after the kingdom is threatened by a mysterious enemy.

Players will explore dangerous environments, fight enemies with a sword and face powerful challenges along the way.

About Ozyrane Games

Ozyrane Games is an independent game development studio focused on creating original games, worlds and stories.

The studio is currently focused on its first project, The Last Blade.

Ozyrane Games is being built around a simple idea: create games with their own atmosphere, characters and worlds while keeping development focused and achievable.
